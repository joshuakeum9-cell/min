Capture integrity probe

Measures whether the system-audio loopback track delivers continuous frames or drops out during silence. The answer decides whether the app needs timestamp gap-reconstruction, or whether frame counts can be trusted as a clock.

Protocol — the point is to create a real silence gap in the system audio while the mic keeps running.
  1. Start something playing through your speakers or headphones (a video, music).
  2. Press Start. Let the audio play for the first third.
  3. Pause the audio completely for the middle third. Keep talking into your mic.
  4. Resume the audio for the final third.
